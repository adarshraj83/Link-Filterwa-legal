Link Filterwa — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Link Filterwa ("Link Filterwa", "Bot", "we", "us", or "our") may access, process, use, and store information when the Bot is added to or used in a Discord server.

Link Filterwa is an independently developed third-party Discord application and is not owned, operated, sponsored, or endorsed by Discord Inc.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled by a server administrator and the permissions granted to the Bot, Link Filterwa may process certain Discord-related information.

This may include:

  • Discord user IDs.
  • Discord usernames, display names, nicknames, and avatars where available through Discord.
  • Server or guild IDs.
  • Channel IDs.
  • Role IDs and relevant permission information.
  • Server configuration and Bot settings.
  • Whitelist and blacklist configuration.
  • Message IDs and author IDs where required for moderation features.
  • URLs, domains, or link-related message content needed for link filtering.
  • Warning, violation, or moderation information generated by the Bot.
  • Information provided directly through commands or other interactions with the Bot.

2. Message Content

Link Filterwa may process message content when required for its link-filtering or moderation functionality.

For example, the Bot may inspect a message to determine whether it contains a URL or domain that matches configured filtering rules.

Message content is processed for the operation of the relevant moderation features and is not intentionally collected for advertising or unrelated profiling.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by Link Filterwa may be used for purposes such as:

  • Detecting and filtering links or domains.
  • Deleting messages that match configured moderation rules.
  • Issuing warnings or recording violations.
  • Applying moderation actions configured by server administrators.
  • Sending moderation or warning notifications.
  • Maintaining whitelist and blacklist settings.
  • Maintaining server and channel configuration.
  • Providing Bot commands and requested functionality.
  • Maintaining security and preventing abuse of the Bot.
  • Diagnosing technical problems and improving reliability.

4. Server Configuration

Server administrators may configure Link Filterwa using available commands and settings. Configuration information may be associated with the relevant Discord server, channels, roles, or users where necessary to provide the configured functionality.

Server administrators are responsible for configuring the Bot appropriately and granting only the permissions necessary for the features they choose to use.

5. Warnings and Moderation Records

If Link Filterwa provides warning, violation, or moderation tracking features, the Bot may process information such as a user's Discord ID, server ID, warning count, violation information, and related moderation data.

This information is used to provide the moderation functionality requested by the server administrator.

6. Logs

Where logging features are enabled, Link Filterwa may send moderation-related information to the logging channel configured by the server administrator.

The contents and visibility of such logs are also affected by Discord's permissions system and the server administrator's configuration.

7. Direct Messages

Link Filterwa may send users direct messages when a feature requires it, such as warning or moderation notifications.

Information used for these notifications is limited to what is reasonably necessary to provide the requested Bot functionality.

8. Data Storage and Retention

Link Filterwa may store configuration, moderation, warning, violation, or other operational information when necessary to provide its features.

The amount and duration of stored information may depend on the feature being used, server configuration, technical requirements, and operational needs.

We do not intend to retain information longer than reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it is processed, unless retention is required for security, legal, or other legitimate operational reasons.

9. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Link Filterwa is not designed to intentionally collect sensitive information such as Discord account passwords, authentication codes, payment card information, or precise physical location.

Users should not provide passwords, authentication codes, payment information, or other sensitive information to the Bot through commands or messages.

10. Sharing of Information

Link Filterwa may rely on third-party services and infrastructure required to operate the Bot.

Information may therefore be processed through:

  • Discord's API and Discord infrastructure.
  • Hosting or infrastructure providers used to operate the Bot.
  • Technical or security services used to maintain the Bot.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Information may also be disclosed where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, protect the security of the service, prevent abuse, or protect the rights and safety of users and others.

11. Discord

Link Filterwa operates through Discord and uses Discord's API. Discord independently processes information according to its own policies and terms.

For information about how Discord handles personal information, please review Discord's Privacy Policy .

12. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures may be used to help protect information processed by Link Filterwa.

However, no internet-based service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Data Deletion Requests

If you believe Link Filterwa has stored information associated with your Discord account and you would like to request deletion, you may contact the developer using the contact information below.

Deletion requests may be subject to technical limitations, legitimate operational requirements, or applicable legal obligations.

14. Server Administrator Responsibility

Server administrators control whether Link Filterwa is added to their server and which permissions and features are enabled.

Administrators are responsible for ensuring that their use of the Bot complies with Discord's policies and applicable laws.

15. Children's Privacy

Link Filterwa is a Discord application and does not intentionally request personal information from users for purposes unrelated to providing its Bot functionality.

Users should use Discord and third-party applications in accordance with Discord's age requirements and applicable laws.

16. International Processing

Depending on the hosting and infrastructure providers used by Link Filterwa, information may be processed or stored in locations outside the country where a user or server is located.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Link Filterwa's features, data practices, infrastructure, or legal requirements change.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
